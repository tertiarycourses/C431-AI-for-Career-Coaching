# C431 Practical Labs

Eight connected coaching tasks use a fictional career transition. Every folder includes a realistic scenario, detailed instructions, copy-ready prompts, prompt PDF, output template and rejoin checkpoint.

- [Lab 01: Consent-Based Career Intake](lab-01-consent-based-intake/README.md) — At fictional Northstar Career Studio, you support Mei’s career transition. Draft a coaching agreement and five neutral intake questions. You will connect the output to the next coaching task, inspect AI assumptions and keep the client in control. The completed artifact is intake-brief.md, ready for a human coaching review.
- [Lab 02: Build a Skills Evidence Ledger](lab-02-evidence-ledger/README.md) — At fictional Northstar Career Studio, you support Mei’s career transition. Translate work examples into an auditable skills inventory. You will connect the output to the next coaching task, inspect AI assumptions and keep the client in control. The completed artifact is skills-ledger.csv, ready for a human coaching review.
- [Lab 03: Compare Career Options](lab-03-career-options/README.md) — At fictional Northstar Career Studio, you support Mei’s career transition. Compare three plausible pathways against Mei’s preferences and evidence. You will connect the output to the next coaching task, inspect AI assumptions and keep the client in control. The completed artifact is options-matrix.csv, ready for a human coaching review.
- [Lab 04: Create Truthful Application Materials](lab-04-truthful-applications/README.md) — At fictional Northstar Career Studio, you support Mei’s career transition. Create a targeted resume summary and cover letter grounded in evidence. You will connect the output to the next coaching task, inspect AI assumptions and keep the client in control. The completed artifact is application-pack.md, ready for a human coaching review.
- [Lab 05: Practise a Coaching Interview](lab-05-interview-rehearsal/README.md) — At fictional Northstar Career Studio, you support Mei’s career transition. Rehearse two answers and improve them with specific feedback. You will connect the output to the next coaching task, inspect AI assumptions and keep the client in control. The completed artifact is interview-log.md, ready for a human coaching review.
- [Lab 06: Review Coaching Records and Bias](lab-06-coaching-records/README.md) — At fictional Northstar Career Studio, you support Mei’s career transition. Produce a minimal coaching summary and check recommendation consistency. You will connect the output to the next coaching task, inspect AI assumptions and keep the client in control. The completed artifact is record-review.md, ready for a human coaching review.
- [Lab 07: Build a 30–60–90 Day Plan](lab-07-action-plan/README.md) — At fictional Northstar Career Studio, you support Mei’s career transition. Turn the preferred career experiment into dated milestones. You will connect the output to the next coaching task, inspect AI assumptions and keep the client in control. The completed artifact is action-plan.csv, ready for a human coaching review.
- [Lab 08: Review a Coaching Pilot](lab-08-programme-review/README.md) — At fictional Northstar Career Studio, you support Mei’s career transition. Interpret a fictional coaching programme and propose a next improvement. You will connect the output to the next coaching task, inspect AI assumptions and keep the client in control.
